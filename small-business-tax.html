Questions about small business taxes

Do California LLCs have extra filing requirements?

Yes. Most California LLCs owe an annual LLC tax to the Franchise Tax Board and may owe an additional fee based on gross receipts, in addition to federal filings. We'll make sure your LLC's filings and payments are on our calendar.

Should I be making quarterly estimated payments?

If you're self-employed and expect to owe tax, you generally need to make estimated payments during the year. We'll calculate recommended amounts and remind you of the due dates.

I'm thinking about forming an LLC or S-corp. Can you help?

We can walk through the tax side of the decision with you on a consultation. For legal formation questions, we recommend also speaking with a business attorney.
