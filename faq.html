FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most. Don't see yours? Ask us directly.

Getting started

How do I get started?

Request a free consultation or call/text 619-764-8026. We'll schedule a short phone or video call, learn about your needs and give you a quote. If you decide to move forward, we'll send an engagement letter and a secure upload link.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The first consultation is free and there's no obligation to hire us.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the complexity of your return, the volume of transactions in your books, and the size of your payroll. You'll receive a clear quote after your free consultation and before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

Do you work with clients outside San Diego?

Yes. We're based in San Diego and work with individuals and businesses throughout California, entirely online.

Working online & security

Do I ever need to visit an office?

No. We're a fully virtual firm. Meetings happen by phone or video and documents are shared electronically.

How do I send my documents?

We'll provide a secure, encrypted upload link. You can upload photos or scans from your phone or computer. Please don't send Social Security numbers, bank account numbers or tax forms as regular email attachments or through our website contact form.

How do you protect my information?

We limit access to client information, use encrypted file sharing, and keep tax return information confidential as required by federal law. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I sign documents electronically?

Yes. Engagement letters and e-file authorizations can be signed electronically where allowed.

Taxes

Do you prepare both federal and California returns?

Yes. We prepare federal returns and California state returns for individuals and small businesses.

What if I receive a letter from the IRS or FTB?

Don't panic, and don't ignore it. Send us a copy through your secure link. Many notices are routine and can be resolved quickly.

Can you help if I haven't filed in a few years?

Yes. We can prepare past-due returns and help you get back on track.

Can I get an extension?

Yes. An extension gives you more time to file, but generally not more time to pay. We can file extensions and estimate any payment due. See our tax deadline calendar.

Bookkeeping & payroll

What software do you use for bookkeeping?

We work in QuickBooks Online. If you're not on it yet, we'll help you get set up.

How behind is too behind?

There's no such thing. Our catch-up bookkeeping service is designed for businesses that are months or years behind.

Can you run payroll for just one or two employees?

Absolutely. Many of our payroll clients are small teams, including S-corp owners paying only themselves.

Still have questions?

We're happy to help. Book a free consultation or give us a call.
