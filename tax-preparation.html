Questions about individual tax returns

What do I need to send you?

After your consultation we'll send a personalized list. Most people need their W-2s and 1099s, last year's return, and records for deductions like mortgage interest, property tax, charitable gifts and childcare. Our tax document checklist is a good place to start.

Can you file prior-year or amended returns?

Yes. We can prepare returns for past years you haven't filed and amend returns that need corrections.

When will I get my refund?

Refund timing is controlled by the IRS and the California Franchise Tax Board. E-filed returns with direct deposit are typically the fastest. You can track federal refunds on IRS.gov with “Where's My Refund?”.
