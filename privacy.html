Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Book SD (“we,” “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal information through this website and in providing tax preparation, bookkeeping and payroll services. It is intended to comply with the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and to describe the privacy rights available to California residents.

Information we collect

Information you give us through this website

When you submit our contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if provided), the type of client you are, the services you're interested in, your preferred contact method and time, how you heard about us, and any message you include. Please do not submit Social Security numbers, financial account numbers or tax documents through the form.

Information from clients

If you become a client, we collect the information needed to provide our services. This may include identification details, Social Security or taxpayer identification numbers, income and tax documents, bank and credit card statements, payroll and employee information, and business financial records. You provide this through secure file-sharing tools after you sign an engagement letter.

Information collected automatically

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or tracking scripts. Our hosting provider may automatically log technical information (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and operations. The optional document checklist saves your check-marks only in your own browser's local storage on your device; that data is never sent to us.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule consultations
  • To provide, bill for and improve our services
  • To prepare and file tax returns, maintain books and process payroll as you authorize
  • To communicate with you about your account, deadlines and services
  • To comply with legal, regulatory and professional obligations and to protect against fraud

Tax return information

Information you provide for tax preparation is protected by federal law, including Internal Revenue Code Section 7216. We do not use or disclose your tax return information for any purpose other than preparing your return, unless you give specific written consent or the disclosure is otherwise permitted by law (for example, to file your return with the IRS and California agencies).

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • With government agencies (such as the IRS, California Franchise Tax Board and EDD) when filing on your behalf
  • With service providers that help us operate, under confidentiality obligations, such as our email provider, secure file-sharing and e-signature tools, accounting and payroll software (such as QuickBooks Online), website hosting (GitHub Pages) and our website form-forwarding service (FormSubmit), which delivers contact-form submissions to our inbox
  • When required by law, subpoena or to protect rights and safety
  • With your direction or consent

How we protect information

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect client information, consistent with the FTC Safeguards Rule and IRS guidance for tax professionals. These include encrypted file sharing, limited access, strong authentication and secure disposal. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please use the secure channels we provide for sensitive documents.

Retention

We keep inquiry information only as long as needed to respond and follow up. We retain client records for as long as needed to provide services and to meet legal and professional record-keeping requirements, then securely dispose of them.

Your California privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, California residents may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use and disclose
  • Access a copy of your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Delete personal information, subject to legal exceptions (for example, records we must keep by law)
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights

Because we do not sell or share personal information or use sensitive personal information for purposes requiring a limit, there is nothing to opt out of. To make a request, email Smartbooksd@gmail.com or call 619-764-8026. We will verify your identity before responding and respond within the time required by law. You may use an authorized agent with your written permission.

California “Shine the Light”: We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Because this website does not track visitors across sites or over time, it does not change its behavior in response to Do Not Track signals. If you use Global Privacy Control, it is honored by default because we do not sell or share personal information.

Text messages

If you agree to receive text messages, we will text only about your inquiry or account. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. We do not share mobile numbers or text-messaging consent with third parties for their marketing.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information through it. Information about minor dependents is collected only from parents or guardians as part of tax services.

Links to other websites

Our site links to government and other websites we don't control. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
