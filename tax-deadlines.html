2027 tax deadline calendar

Key federal and California filing and payment due dates for calendar-year taxpayers, adjusted for weekends.

Please note: Dates shown are for calendar-year filers and move to the next business day when they fall on a weekend. Holidays, disaster relief and fiscal-year filing can change your actual due dates. Always confirm with us, IRS.gov or FTB.ca.gov.
Filter deadlines by taxpayer type
  1. Jan15Fri · 2027

    Q4 2026 estimated tax payment

    • Federal 4th-quarter estimated tax (Form 1040-ES)
    • California 4th estimated payment (Form 540-ES)
    IndividualsSelf-employed
  2. Feb1Mon · 2027

    W-2s, 1099-NECs & Q4 payroll returns

    • Furnish W-2s to employees and file with the SSA
    • Furnish and file 1099-NEC for contractors
    • Federal Form 941 (Q4) and Form 940 (annual FUTA)
    • California EDD DE 9 and DE 9C (Q4)
    BusinessesEmployers
  3. Mar15Mon · 2027

    Partnership & S-corp returns

    • Form 1065 (partnerships & multi-member LLCs)
    • Form 1120-S (S corporations)
    • California Form 565 / 568 / 100S as applicable
    • Deadline to request a 6-month extension
    Businesses
  4. Apr15Thu · 2027

    Individual tax day + Q1 estimates

    • Federal Form 1040 and California Form 540 for 2026
    • Deadline to file an extension (to October 15)
    • Q1 2027 estimated tax, federal and California
    • C-corporation returns (Form 1120 / CA 100) for calendar-year filers
    • California LLC annual tax (Form 3522) for calendar-year LLCs
    • Single-member LLC Form 568 (owned by an individual)
    IndividualsSelf-employedBusinesses

    • Federal Form 941 (Q1)
    • California EDD DE 9 and DE 9C (Q1)

    • California Q2 estimated tax (CA has no September payment)
    IndividualsSelf-employed

    • Federal Form 941 (Q2)
    • California EDD DE 9 and DE 9C (Q2)

    Q3 estimates + extended business returns

    • Extended C-corporation returns (Form 1120)
