Questions about payroll

I'm hiring my first employee. Where do I start?

Book a consultation. We'll walk you through the registrations you'll need with the IRS and California EDD, the new-hire paperwork, and choosing a pay schedule, then set it all up for you.

Can you pay independent contractors too?

Yes. We can handle contractor payments and prepare 1099-NEC forms at year end.

What if I've already been running payroll myself?

No problem. We'll review what's been filed so far and take over going forward, making sure the year's totals line up.
