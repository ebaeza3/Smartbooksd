FAQ

Common questions

Do I ever need to come into an office?

No. Smart Book SD is 100% online. We meet by phone or video, and you share documents through a secure upload link, so you can work with us from anywhere in California.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The first call is free and there's no obligation. We'll talk through what you need and give you a clear quote before any work begins.

How do I send my documents?

After your consultation, we'll send you a secure link to upload tax forms, bank statements and receipts. Please don't send sensitive documents like Social Security numbers by regular email.

Do you only work with clients in San Diego?

We're based in San Diego, but because everything is virtual we serve individuals and businesses throughout California.

I'm months behind on my bookkeeping. Can you help?

Absolutely. Catch-up and cleanup bookkeeping is one of our specialties. We'll get your books current and set you up so it doesn't happen again.
