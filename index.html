    FAQ

    Common questions

    Do I ever need to come into an office?

    No. Smart Book SD is 100% online. We meet by phone or video and you share documents through a secure, encrypted upload link, so you can work with us from anywhere in California.

    Is the consultation really free?

    Yes. Your first consultation is free with no obligation. We'll learn about your situation and give you a clear quote before any work begins.

    How do I send my documents securely?

    After your consultation we'll send you a secure upload link. Please don't email Social Security numbers, bank account numbers or tax documents as regular email attachments.

    Do you only work with clients in San Diego?

    We're based in San Diego, but because everything is virtual we serve individuals and businesses throughout California.

    I'm months behind on my bookkeeping. Can you help?

    Absolutely. Catch-up and cleanup bookkeeping is one of our specialties. We'll get your books current and set you up so it doesn't happen again.
