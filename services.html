Individual Tax Returns

We prepare accurate federal and California returns and look for every deduction and credit you qualify for. Upload your documents securely, and we'll take it from there, with a review call before anything is filed.

  • Federal (Form 1040) and California (540) returns
  • W-2, 1099, investment and rental income
  • Itemized vs. standard deduction review
  • Dependents, education and child credits
  • E-filing with direct deposit
  • Prior-year and amended returns

Small Business Taxes

Self-employed or running a small business? We prepare your business return and make sure your expenses, home office, vehicle and other deductions are claimed correctly, so you keep more of what you earn.

  • Schedule C for sole proprietors and freelancers
  • Single-member and multi-member LLCs

Monthly Bookkeeping (QuickBooks)

We keep your QuickBooks Online file current every month: categorizing transactions, reconciling accounts and sending you clear reports, so you always know where your business stands and tax time is easy.

  • Transaction categorization and bank feed review
  • Bank and credit card reconciliations
  • Monthly profit & loss and balance sheet
  • QuickBooks Online setup and chart of accounts
  • Receipt and document management
  • Year-end package ready for your tax return
