Accessibility Statement

Updated November 18, 2026

Smart Book SD is committed to making our website usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we've done

  • Semantic headings, landmarks and a “skip to main content” link
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators
  • Text and interface colors chosen for sufficient contrast
  • Labels and error messages on every form field
  • Layouts that adapt to phones, tablets, zoom up to 200% and larger text
  • Animations that turn off automatically if your device is set to reduce motion

Need help or found a problem?

If any part of this site is difficult to use, please let us know and we'll provide the information in another way and work to fix it. Contact us at Smartbooksd@gmail.com or 619-764-8026. Because we serve clients entirely by phone, video and email, we're happy to accommodate the format that works best for you.
