About Us

A modern, online approach to your finances

Smart Book SD is a San Diego–based tax, bookkeeping and payroll firm that works with clients entirely online.

Our approach

We started Smart Book SD to make professional tax and bookkeeping help simple and accessible. No office visits, no waiting rooms and no shoebox of receipts. Just clear communication and accurate work.

Because we're 100% virtual, we can focus our time where it matters: getting your numbers right, keeping you compliant and helping you understand where your money is going.

Whether you're filing a personal return, running an LLC, or finally getting your books caught up, we'll treat your finances with the same care we'd give our own.
